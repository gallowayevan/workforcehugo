Direction A — Data Forward (restrained)

Internal review page (not indexed, not linked). The safest evolution of the current site: everything below is built only from existing brand assets — the four logo colors and the ColorBrewer ramps already used in the county maps. Compare with Direction B and Direction C.

Principles

The differentiator is the data: 45+ years of licensure records and real visualization capability. So the design is data-forward — the most distinctive element on any page should be a number, a chart, or North Carolina's geography, never decoration. Identity comes from disciplined use of the logo palette and strong typography, not from trends. Flat surfaces, hierarchy from size and weight, whitespace with a purpose.

Deliberately excluded (the generic-AI look and the generic-corporate look)

  • No cream/beige backgrounds, no rust-orange accents
  • No italicized display serifs, no letterspaced all-caps subheadings
  • No glowing cards, no rounded-rectangle-in-rounded-rectangle dashboards, no ticker bars
  • No stock photography of stethoscopes or handshakes

Palette

Core colors come straight from the logo. Data ramps are the ColorBrewer greens/purples the maps already use — the site chrome and the charts finally share a family. Contrast ratios given against white; all pass WCAG AA for their role.

Typography — two options, pick one

Both are open source, self-hostable, and have strong tabular numerals for stats. Current site uses the OS default font (Segoe on Windows), which is most of why it reads "unstyled."
