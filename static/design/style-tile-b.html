DESIGN DIRECTION B · STYLE TILE · INTERNAL REVIEW

The Almanac

Leans into the thing no consultancy can imitate: 45+ years of continuous printed record. The reference: state data almanacs, USGS survey bulletins, the long tradition of government statistical publications — done with modern craft. Hairline rules, numbered figures, a working table of contents. Quietly confident, built to be cited.

SEC. 01

Palette — ink on paper

White paper (deliberately not cream), near-black ink, and the two logo colors doing all accent work. Data ramps unchanged from the county maps. Color is information here, never decoration.

SEC. 02

Typography — Bitter + Source Sans 3 + Plex Mono

A slab serif with printed-report bones for display (upright and bold, never italic), a clear humanist sans for reading, and a monospace reserved for figure numbers, data values, and sources — the almanac's "typewriter" voice.

SEC. 04

Listings — an index, not a card wall

Blog and report listings become a catalog: numbered entries with dates, titles, one-line summaries, and a small inline figure where it earns its place. Denser, faster to scan than image cards, and no unreadable thumbnail screenshots.
