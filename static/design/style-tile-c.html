Civic Editorial

The boldest of the three: newsroom-data-desk energy (think ProPublica, The Pudding, a modern statehouse bureau). One typeface — Libre Franklin, the civic Franklin Gothic lineage — at extreme weight contrast, a deep navy derived from the brand blue, and charts doing the talking at hero scale. For engaging policymakers and the public who scroll past quiet.

Palette — duotone plus one

Navy (a dark shade of the logo blue) and the logo green carry everything; the map purple appears only as the interactive-content marker. White text on navy is 12.6:1; the bright green is reserved for large text and graphics on navy.

Typography — one family, extreme contrast

Libre Franklin only: Black (900) for headlines and numbers, Semibold for structure, Regular for reading. No second typeface — scale and weight do all the work.
